About me

I am a researcher at the Institute of Theoretical Astrophysics and the Rosseland Centre for Solar Physics, University of Oslo, and principal investigator of a Research Council of Norway Talentutland grant. I study how the Sun's chromosphere and corona are heated, combining observations from IRIS, SDO and the Swedish 1-m Solar Telescope with radiative-MHD simulations.

I also build machine-learning methods for solar data and lead data-modelling work for NASA's MUSE mission, which launches in 2027.

Alongside this I'm also interested in AI interpretability. As part of the SPAR Spring 2026 cohort I study where in a language model fine-tuning should happen, and what the model forgets when it happens in the wrong place.

Before Oslo I spent nearly four years as a Research Scientist for NASA's IRIS and MUSE missions at the Lockheed Martin Solar & Astrophysics Laboratory. I received my PhD from the University of Oslo in 2021.
